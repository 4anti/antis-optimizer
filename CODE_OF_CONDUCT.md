# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want `antis-optimizer` to be a harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Own mistakes, apologize, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer, [@4anti](https://github.com/4anti), through the contact options on that GitHub profile, or use GitHub's built-in **Report content** option on the offending comment. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
